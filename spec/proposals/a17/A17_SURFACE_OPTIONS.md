# A17 — Surface Options Considered

## Selected — Option A: general `מעלה` profile

**Shape:** a fixed generic noun `מעלה`, counted `מעלות`, with `מעלת היתד` as the distinguished origin.

**Why selected:** Biblical `מעלה` is directly a step/stair/grade, and 2 Kings 20:9–11 / Isaiah 38:8 use counted `מעלות` as a scale on which motion can reverse by ten steps. That is unusually close to the semantic shape of a discrete ordered coordinate. The noun is feminine, so the existing A15 feminine count morphology composes naturally.

The origin phrase `מעלת היתד` is a controlled composition, not a claimed attestation. `יתד` is a Biblical peg/anchor metaphor and the Megillah itself says the foundation day is a peg from which surrounding days are measured. It avoids implying that the origin is the beginning of the line.

## Rejected — Option B: reusable arbitrary noun/profile family

Example concept: permit source to supply a noun such as “year”, “day”, or another noun to a general Index template.

Rejected because:
- arbitrary nouns would become type-like profile arguments;
- the compiler would need an unaudited open class of unit morphology/agreement;
- it would create expected-context/profile inference pressure;
- “day” would be one accidental instance of a generic unit system that B13 never specified;
- the lexical and semantic cost is much larger than D4-LANG-001 requires.

## Rejected — `מעמד`

`מעמד` is Biblical and can mean station/post/position (e.g. Isaiah 22:19), but its primary attested uses are office, assigned station, or standing place. It is not attested as a countable discrete two-sided scale. It describes a position well but does not explain the required exact step distance or neighboring progression.

## Rejected — `צעד`

`צעד` is strongly Biblical and countable; Job 31:4 says that all the speaker's steps are counted. It denotes a pace/step **taken**, however, so it naturally models traversal or distance rather than the identity of a stationary coordinate. A17 uses that distinction to keep “count the distance” algorithmic rather than making `צעד` a value type.

## Rejected — `מנין`

`מנין` is not a good Controlled Biblical Hebrew basis here. Its sole biblical occurrence is in the Aramaic of Ezra 6:17, where it means enumeration/number. It would also pull the surface toward Natural enumeration instead of a two-sided coordinate.

## Rejected — `מקום`

`מקום` would be period-authentic as “place”, but Marak already grants it a central and frozen state-holder ontology. Reusing it as the generic Index head would create a direct source-ontology collision.

## Rejected origin — `מעלת היסוד`

Both words are historically defensible, and the Megillah says `יום היסוד`. Nevertheless “foundation” can naturally suggest a beginning or lower base, while D4 expressly requires values on both sides and the Megillah explicitly denies that the foundation day is the beginning of all days. `מעלת היתד` better expresses a fixed reference mark without a lower-bound implication.

## Rejected origin — `מעלת אין`

This would expand A15's tightly scoped `אין` beyond `שנת אין`, and the composition has no established Biblical sense corresponding to a coordinate origin. A17 does not generalize `אין`.
