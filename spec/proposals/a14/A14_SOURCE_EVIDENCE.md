## Biblical-Hebrew evidence

### Strict comparison
- Deuteronomy 20:1 — `עם רב ממך`.
- 1 Kings 19:7 — `כי רב ממך הדרך`.

These support the comparative valency `X רב מן Y`.

### Large numerals
- Numbers 17:14 — `ארבעה עשר אלף ושבע מאות`.
- 1 Chronicles 21:5 — `אלף אלפים ומאה אלף`.

These support thousand composition and the `אלף אלפים` million scale.

### Counted action
- Genesis 33:3 — `וישתחו ארצה שבע פעמים`.
- 2 Kings 13:19 — `שלש פעמים תכה ...` and `חמש או שש פעמים`.

These establish `פעמים` as ordinary action-count language. A14 nevertheless uses a narrower canonical
attachment for Marak.

### Ordered book membership
- Daniel 12:1 — `כל הנמצא כתוב בספר`.

This supports `כתוב בספר` as an ordinary membership/record relation, but does not by itself settle the
runtime collection semantics.

## Evidence policy

An attested phrase demonstrates linguistic possibility, not automatically the full Marak semantics.
Every computational relation remains explicitly specified in the proposal.
