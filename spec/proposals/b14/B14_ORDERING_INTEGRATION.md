# B14 Ordering Integration

A15 `A רב מן B` maps uniquely to B13 Natural GT(A,B). Less-than uses reversed operands.
The result is a proposition consumed by control, never a Boolean Value.

No mixed Natural/BidirectionalIndex comparison is admitted.

B13 LE/GE remain derived meta-semantic relations. A15 correctly exposes no direct LE/GE. A source
needing a non-strict Natural condition may compose frozen control/equality with strict order.

Collection ordering consumes only admitted strict total relations: Natural order, validated explicit
Symbol-domain order, or Lex(R). No comparator callback, declaration-order fallback, or spelling order.
