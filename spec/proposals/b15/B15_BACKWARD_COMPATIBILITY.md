# B15 Backward Compatibility

B15 is a conservative semantic integration over frozen A13/B12 and reviewed A15/B13.

Unchanged:
- Natural state, roles, output and equality retain their existing meanings;
- place identity/content distinction remains explicit;
- named role association remains occurrence-local and immutable;
- output remains nonterminal and zero/one;
- B12 provenance lifetime/error remains;
- Natural subtraction underflow remains Natural-domain failure;
- no Boolean Value appears;
- A15 Program Input, Collection, numeral, ordering and recurrence semantics are unchanged.

A16's typed carriers are additional explicitly distinguished source forms; they do not reinterpret an
accepted A13 phrase as generic polymorphism.
