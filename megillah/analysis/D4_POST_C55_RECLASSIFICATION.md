## Whole-program structure

The historical `ועתה` near the old “large number” section remains ordinary historical wording, not the principal transition by privilege of being the only lexical occurrence.

The final query workflow (original 1808ff) is still the strongest **semantic** principal-computation candidate because it explicitly takes the two inputs, finds the target year and derives the five requested fields. This conclusion comes from the algorithmic discourse, not its Markdown heading. No D4 source rewrite places the final `ועתה` while the day-value blocker remains unresolved.

## Five-result strategy

No tuple/multi-return feature is requested.

The five fields can be retained in five typed referents:
1. year number — BidirectionalIndex;
2. cutlet name — Symbol(cutlet-domain);
3. day in cutlet — Natural;
4. month name — Symbol(month-domain);
5. day in month — Natural.

A D4 production proof verifies that these five heterogeneous typed facts coexist and are observed identically by HAST reference, IR reference, and portable backend. This is a structural result strategy, not yet the full final-query implementation.
